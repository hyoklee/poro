# Concept: Brady pumping compared with the Pohang EGS stimulation

## Definition

This page compares the fluid injection at Brady Hot Springs during PoroTomo (March 2016) with the hydraulic stimulation at the Pohang enhanced geothermal system (EGS) that preceded the 2017 M 5.4 earthquake. It asks whether Brady's operations resemble the conditions that triggered Pohang. Short answer: the trigger mechanism and the post-shutdown timing are alike, but the volume balance, purpose and rock response are very different. Per cubic metre injected, Brady is thousands of times less seismogenic.

## What they have in common

- **Same mechanism.** Both inject water into fractured rock at a geothermal site, raising pore pressure on nearby faults ([concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md](concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md)).
- **Seismicity after injection stops.** Pohang's mainshock came on 2017-11-15, about two months after the last injection. At Brady all 5 catalogued events of March 2016 (ML −0.48 to 0.07) fall inside the full-field shutdown of 2016-03-13 21:17 to 03-18 12:12 UTC ([source-brady-meq-catalog-gdr859.md](source-brady-meq-catalog-gdr859.md), [source-brady-pumping-gdr917.md](source-brady-pumping-gdr917.md)), consistent with Cardiff et al. (2018, EPSL 482). At both sites the hazard window outlasts the pumping.

## How they differ

| | Pohang (2016–17) | Brady (2016-03-11 to 03-27, GDR 917) |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Hydraulic stimulation of granite at about 4.2 km to create permeability | Operation of an existing hydrothermal field |
| Injected volume | About 12,800 m³ over about 20 months | 465,448 m³ in 15.96 days |
| Net fluid balance | Injection only | 505,933 m³ extracted, net −40,485 m³ |
| Seismogenic index Σ | About −2 to −1 | −5.45 (b = 1), −5.32 (b = 0.73), −5.28 (b = 0.65) |
| Largest event | M 3.1 during stimulation, then M 5.4 | ML 0.07 |
| Fault | Unmapped, critically stressed fault next to PX-2 | None identified in the sources of this wiki |

Sources: [concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md](concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md), [source-shapiro-2021-pohang.md](source-shapiro-2021-pohang.md), [agent_induced_seismicity.md](agent_induced_seismicity.md).

What follows from the table:

- **Volume alone is not the hazard.** Brady injected about 36 times Pohang's total in 16 days and produced nothing above ML 0.1. The difference lies in how readily the rock fails (Σ) and whether a large, critically stressed fault is close by.
- **Σ is 3.3 to 4.4 log units lower at Brady**, so a given magnitude is crossed roughly 2,000 to 25,000 times less often per cubic metre injected.
- **Brady is net-producing.** Pohang's injection was one-way. (That a balanced field does not build up reservoir pressure the way one-way injection does is general reasoning, not a number from these sources.)

## Probability

With Shapiro, Kim & Ree (2021) Eq. 5 ([concept-seismogenic-index.md](concept-seismogenic-index.md)), the 2026-11-15 agent run gives about 1 × 10⁻⁴ per year for Mw ≥ 5.5 at Brady under PoroTomo-era pumping (b = 1). The same code with Pohang's parameters gives 1.2 % to 29 %, which brackets the published 15–17 % worst case.

Caveats: the Brady Σ rests on 5 events (about ±0.4 log units from counting alone), and the model extrapolates Gutenberg–Richter statistics without knowing whether Brady has a fault large enough to host an Mw 5.5.
