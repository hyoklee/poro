# Source: Shapiro, Kim & Ree (2021), Nature Communications 12:6397

"Magnitude and nucleation time of the 2017 Pohang Earthquake point to its predictable artificial triggering." Open access: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8568929/ (read 2026-11-15).

## Numbers used in this wiki

- Pohang mainshock: Mw 5.5. The Korean agencies report M 5.4, which is the value [concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md](concept-induced-seismicity-pohang.md) uses.
- Seismogenic index at Pohang: "approximately between −2 and −1".
- b-values: 0.65 and 0.73, "the two known realistic values".
- Volume in their Fig. 3c Pohang case: about 5,850 m³. Basel comparison: about 11,570 m³.
- Exceedance probability, Eq. 5: W = 1 − exp[−V_f / 10^(b·M − Σ)], written in this wiki as 1 − exp(−V·10^(Σ − bM)).
- Worst-case probability of the Pohang event: 15–17 %. Stopping after the Mw 2.3 event would have kept it at about 3 % or less.
- Magnitudes plotted for Pohang: Mw ≥ 0.9. The paper gives no formal completeness magnitude.

## Counterpoint

Westaway (2021, Geothermics) argues many Pohang magnitudes were underestimated, that b is about 1.12 for one subset, and so the predicted probability was much lower.
