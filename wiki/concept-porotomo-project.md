# Concept: the PoroTomo project

## Definition

PoroTomo ("Poroelastic Tomography by Adjoint Inverse Modeling of Data from Seismology, Geodesy, and Hydrology") was a DOE Geothermal Technologies Office project (EE0006760) led by the University of Wisconsin. In March 2016 it turned the Brady Hot Springs geothermal field near Fernley, Nevada into a natural laboratory: the operator changed injection and production on a schedule while the team recorded strain and seismic signals with trenched and downhole DAS fiber, nodal seismometers, a vibroseis truck as an active source, and InSAR for surface deformation.

## Why

Brady's field had wells that lost productivity as the reservoir changed. The project tested whether the subsurface flow paths could be imaged without drilling, by inverting the poroelastic response to pumping together with seismic velocity. The method targets the main cost of geothermal: drilling into the wrong rock.
