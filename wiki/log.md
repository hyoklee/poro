## [2026-11-15] test | Induced-seismicity anomaly agent on 759 DASV records (PBS, Aurora)

Built the agent from `claude.md`: `agent/` holds the manifest builder, detector, hazard model, PBS scripts, clio-coder interpretation script and a clio-agent blueprint. Ingested three new sources: GDR 917 pumping CSV, GDR 859 LBNL catalogue (5 events), and Shapiro, Kim & Ree 2021. PBS job 9092130 (`IOWarp`, `debug`, 2 nodes × 24 ranks) analysed every 20th DASV record (759 of 15,164) in 3 min 12 s wall time. 6 proxy SSL download failures were rerun on the login node, so all 759 are ok. Results: 57 anomalous records, 62 coherent detections, 47 of 62 in local daytime. Spearman correlation with pumping is at most |0.17|. The second shutdown shows 0.21 detections per record against 0.18 for the same hours on other days (P = 0.43), so there is no pumping signal. All 5 catalogue events fall in the 03-13 to 03-18 shutdown, before DASV recording starts. Seismogenic index Σ is −5.45, −5.32 or −5.28 for b = 1, 0.73 or 0.65. P(Mw ≥ 5.5) is 1.2e-4 per year with b = 1, against 1.2–29 % recomputed for Pohang. The DAS-based uncalibrated ceiling is Σ = −2.70 and 7.8e-2 per year. clio-coder's code review was useful. Its interpretation had 3 errors, now annotated in the file. The clio-agent blueprint was not executed because the server could not get an ALCF Globus token. Filed as `agent_induced_seismicity.md`, `concept-seismogenic-index.md` and three `source-*` pages. Schema extended with `agent_*.md`.
